What is Kerjiva Malaysia?

Kerjiva Malaysia is a career platform that connects jobseekers with companies in one simple, modern, and easy-to-use place.

For jobseekers, Kerjiva helps users discover job opportunities, explore vacancy information, submit applications, and follow their application progress.

For companies, Kerjiva provides a platform to introduce their businesses, publish job vacancies, review applicants, and manage recruitment activities more easily.

Kerjiva Services

Kerjiva provides services designed to meet the needs of both jobseekers and companies in one platform.

For Jobseekers

  • Search for job vacancies by position and location.
  • Explore complete job vacancy information.
  • Submit job applications.
  • Manage profiles and uploaded documents.
  • Monitor application progress.
  • View interview schedule information.

For Companies

  • Create a company profile.
  • Publish job vacancies.
  • Manage published and draft job listings.
  • Review and manage incoming applications.
  • Contact potential candidates.
  • Invite candidates for interviews.

Benefits of Kerjiva for Jobseekers and Companies

Kerjiva is designed to help jobseekers discover employment opportunities while helping companies manage their recruitment needs through one platform. Each feature aims to make job searching and application management more practical and organised.

Benefits for Jobseekers

Jobseekers can use Kerjiva to manage important steps in their career search.

  • Explore job vacancies by position and location.
  • Review job requirements and details before applying.
  • Submit applications through the available platform features.
  • Complete and update profile information and documents.
  • Monitor available application progress information.
  • Check interview schedule information when available.

Benefits for Companies

Kerjiva provides companies with tools to present their business information and manage recruitment activities more efficiently.

  • Introduce company profiles and information to jobseekers.
  • Create and manage job vacancy listings.
  • Review applications submitted through the platform.
  • Manage candidate information and application processes.
  • Use available communication features to contact candidates.
  • Manage interview information when the relevant features are used.
