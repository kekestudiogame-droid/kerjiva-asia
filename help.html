Help Center

Kerjiva Malaysia

Find answers to common questions and learn how to use Kerjiva Malaysia.

1. General Questions

What is Kerjiva Malaysia?

Kerjiva Malaysia is a career platform that connects jobseekers with companies and helps users discover suitable job opportunities.

Is Kerjiva Malaysia free to use?

Users can create an account and explore available job opportunities through the platform.

2. Jobseeker Help

How do I create a jobseeker account?

Select the registration option, complete the required information, and verify your email address.

How do I apply for a job?

Browse available jobs, open a job listing, and select Apply Now. Follow the instructions to submit your application and required documents.

Can I update my profile and documents?

Yes. You can manage your profile and uploaded documents through your Jobseeker Dashboard.

3. Company Help

How do I register as a company?

Select company registration, complete the required details, and verify your email address.

How do I publish a job vacancy?

Sign in to your Company Dashboard, create and save a job listing as a draft, then select the publish option when it is ready.

How do I manage applications?

You can review and manage applications through your Company Dashboard.

4. Account & Login

I have not received my verification email. What should I do?

Check your inbox and spam folder. Make sure you entered the correct email address. If the problem continues, contact our support team.

What if I forget my password?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.
