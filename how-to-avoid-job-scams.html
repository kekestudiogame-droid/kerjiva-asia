How to Avoid Job Scams

Learn how to identify fake job vacancies and protect yourself throughout your job search.

Finding a job requires careful consideration. With so many job opportunities available, job seekers need to be cautious of unclear or potentially fraudulent offers. By recognising the warning signs, you can protect yourself and explore career opportunities more safely.

1. Recognise the Signs of Fake Job Vacancies

Look out for the following warning signs before applying for a job:

  • The company's information and recruiter's identity are unclear.
  • Job descriptions and requirements are incomplete or poorly explained.
  • The job offers unrealistic salaries or benefits without a clear explanation of the role.
  • The recruitment process seems unusually easy and guarantees employment.
  • The recruiter uses an email address or contact number that cannot be verified as an official company contact.

2. Be Careful of Requests for Payment

Be cautious if someone asks you to make payments for administrative fees, training, uniforms, travel expenses, or job placement guarantees.

Do not rush into making any payments. Verify the company's identity and the authenticity of the recruitment information before making a decision.

3. Protect Your Personal Information

Your personal information and important documents must be protected throughout the job application process. Keep the following precautions in mind:

  • Never share your password or OTP (One-Time Password) with anyone.
  • Do not send personal documents to unverified individuals or organisations.
  • Make sure you understand why your documents and personal information are being requested.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links sent by unknown parties.

4. Verify the Company's Identity

Before applying, take the time to check the company's information through official sources.

  • Check the company's official website and profiles.
  • Verify the company's address and contact information.
  • Make sure the job vacancy matches the position advertised by the company.
  • If you have any doubts, contact the company through its official communication channels.

5. What Should You Do If You Encounter a Job Scam?

If you come across a suspicious job vacancy or someone you believe may be involved in a scam, take the following steps:

  • Stop communicating with the party if you notice suspicious signs.
  • Do not send money or provide any additional personal information.
  • Keep evidence of communications, messages, and job vacancy details.
  • Report the incident to the platform where you found the vacancy or to the relevant authorities through their official channels.

6. Kerjiva's Role

Kerjiva aims to help job seekers discover job vacancies and career opportunities through the Kerjiva platform. We encourage all users to carefully verify job information, protect their personal data, and remain cautious of suspicious offers.

Conclusion

Being careful when choosing job opportunities is an important step in protecting yourself from scams. Do not rush into making decisions, and always verify the information before applying for a job.
