How to Post a Job on Kerjiva

Easy, free, and reach more talent with Kerjiva.

Kerjiva helps companies find suitable candidates for their recruitment needs. Through the Kerjiva platform, companies can post job vacancies and manage candidate applications more easily.

1. Register as a Company

Visit the Kerjiva Malaysia website at my.kerjiva.com, then select the Register menu. Choose the Company account type and complete the required registration information.

Make sure you use an active email address for the account verification process.

2. Complete Your Company Profile

After successfully logging in, complete your company profile so jobseekers can learn more about your organisation.

Provide accurate and clear company information, including your company name and other relevant details.

3. Post a Job Vacancy

Open your company dashboard, then select the feature to create a job vacancy.

Complete the job information, including:

  • Job title or position.
  • Job location.
  • Employment type.
  • Salary range, if available.
  • Job description and requirements.

Make sure all the information is clear and relevant to your company's requirements.

4. Publish Your Job Vacancy

Review all the information before publishing the vacancy. Once everything is correct, proceed with the publishing process so the job vacancy can be displayed to jobseekers on Kerjiva.

5. Manage Candidate Applications

Companies can monitor incoming applications through the company dashboard. Review candidate profiles and documents to support your recruitment and selection process.

Tips for Creating a Good Job Vacancy

  • Use a clear job title.
  • Write a complete job description.
  • Include relevant job requirements.
  • Provide salary and location information when available.
  • Make sure the job information is always accurate.
