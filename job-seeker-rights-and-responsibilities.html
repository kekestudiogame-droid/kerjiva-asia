Job Seeker Rights and Responsibilities

Understand your rights and responsibilities to make your job search safer, more comfortable, and professional.

Finding a job is not just about discovering suitable vacancies. It is also important to understand your rights and responsibilities as a job seeker. Knowing both can help you navigate the recruitment process more wisely and confidently.

1. Job Seeker Rights

Every job seeker deserves a fair, transparent, and professional recruitment process. Here are some important things to be aware of:

  • Clear job information: Access information about the position, job responsibilities, location, and requirements.
  • Fair treatment: Have the opportunity to participate in the selection process without discrimination.
  • Selection process information: Receive explanations about recruitment stages in accordance with company policies.
  • Personal data security: Personal information and documents submitted during the application process should be kept confidential.

2. Job Seeker Responsibilities

Along with having rights, job seekers should also fulfil their responsibilities throughout the job application process:

  • Provide accurate information: Ensure that your CV, work experience, education, and skills reflect your actual background.
  • Prepare the required documents: Provide the documents requested by the company according to the position requirements.
  • Maintain professional communication: Use polite language and respond appropriately when communicating with company representatives.
  • Follow the selection process: Arrive on time and respect interview schedules and other selection stages.

3. Tips for a Smarter Job Search

To help avoid unpleasant or potentially harmful experiences, keep the following tips in mind:

  • Verify the company's identity and information before applying.
  • Read job descriptions and requirements carefully.
  • Be cautious of unclear payment requests during the recruitment process.
  • Avoid sharing personal information or important documents carelessly.
  • Use job search platforms that provide clear and reliable vacancy information.

4. Kerjiva's Role

Kerjiva is a career platform that helps job seekers discover job vacancies and career opportunities. We encourage users to stay informed, protect their personal data, and use the platform responsibly.

Conclusion

Understanding your rights and responsibilities is an important step towards a safe and professional job search. Be an active, honest, and responsible job seeker when exploring career opportunities.
