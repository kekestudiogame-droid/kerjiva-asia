ABOUT KERJIVA

Why Choose Kerjiva? A Career Platform for Job Seekers and Companies in Malaysia

Get to know Kerjiva, a Malaysian career platform that helps job seekers discover job opportunities and companies manage their recruitment process through various features in one platform.

Finding a job and identifying the right candidates are two important parts of the world of work. Job seekers need access to relevant job vacancy information, while companies need a practical way to introduce job opportunities and reach potential employees.

Kerjiva is here as a Malaysian career platform that brings the needs of job seekers and companies together in one place.

Through its available features, Kerjiva aims to help job seekers discover career opportunities and help companies manage job vacancy information and candidate recruitment.

Kerjiva is developed to make job searching and recruitment management easier to access on computers and mobile devices.

Benefits of Kerjiva for Job Seekers

For job seekers, finding a suitable position involves more than browsing a list of vacancies. Users also need a place to manage their profiles, prepare documents, submit applications, and follow the progress of the recruitment process.

1. Find Job Vacancy Information

Kerjiva provides a job search feature that helps users explore opportunities posted by companies on the platform. Job seekers can review job details and consider whether each position matches their interests and qualifications.

2. Manage Your Profile

Users can complete their personal profiles as part of their job application preparation. Profile information helps companies learn about the background and details of applicants. An up-to-date and accurately completed profile can help job seekers present their information in a more organised way.

3. Store and Manage Documents

Kerjiva provides a My Documents feature to help users store and manage documents needed when applying for jobs. Keeping documents in one place makes it easier for job seekers to prepare the files required when submitting an application.

4. Manage Job Applications

Through the My Applications feature, users can view the list of job applications they have submitted. This feature helps job seekers monitor their application activity without relying on separate records.

5. Receive Interview Invitation Information

Recruitment may proceed to the interview stage. Kerjiva provides an Interview Invitations feature that allows job seekers to view interview invitations sent by companies. This information helps users keep track of interview schedules and important details.

6. Share Experiences Through the Community

The world of work is about more than finding and securing a job. Experiences, knowledge, and stories shared by other people can also be valuable sources of learning.

Through the Community feature, users can share experiences, stories, questions, and opinions about the world of work. This feature is intended to provide a space where users can exchange information and build positive connections within the career community.

Benefits of Kerjiva for Companies

In addition to helping job seekers, Kerjiva is also developed to support companies in introducing job opportunities and managing recruitment activities.

1. Post Job Vacancies

Companies can use the Post a Job feature to create and manage job vacancy information. Clearly presented job details can help potential candidates understand the position, requirements, and qualifications needed.

2. Manage Job Vacancies

Through the My Jobs feature, companies can view and manage the vacancies they have created. This helps companies monitor job listings that are still being prepared as well as those that have already been published.

3. View Applicants

Kerjiva provides an Incoming Applicants feature that helps companies view candidates who have applied for their job vacancies. With application information organised on the platform, companies can review candidates according to their recruitment needs.

4. Manage Application Documents

Documents submitted by job seekers can help companies understand candidates' information and qualifications. Kerjiva supports the management of application documents so companies can review files related to their recruitment process.

5. Send Interview Invitations

When a company wants to move forward with its selection process, the Send Interview Invitation feature can be used to invite candidates to an interview. This feature helps companies share interview schedules and details through the platform.

Easy Access to Kerjiva

Kerjiva is designed as a web-based platform that can be accessed using a desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or mobile phone with an internet connection.

With a layout that adapts to different screen sizes, users can access various parts of the platform according to their needs and the devices they use. This accessibility is intended to help job seekers and companies use Kerjiva more flexibly.

Kerjiva supports both needs through one platform offering various features, from job searches and profile and document management to application tracking and interview invitation information. For companies, Kerjiva also provides tools to post vacancies and manage recruitment activities.
