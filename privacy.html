Privacy Policy

Kerjiva Malaysia

Last Updated: 18 November 2026

Kerjiva Malaysia ("Kerjiva", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

Kerjiva Malaysia is currently operated independently as a privately managed platform and is not represented as a registered company.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you when using the Kerjiva Malaysia website and services.

By accessing or using Kerjiva Malaysia, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name and email address.
  • Account credentials and authentication-related information.
  • Account type, such as Jobseeker or Company.

Jobseeker Information

  • Personal and contact details provided in your profile.
  • Education, employment history, skills, and other professional information.
  • Documents uploaded to your account, such as CVs, resumes, certificates, and other supporting documents.

Company Information

  • Company name and contact details.
  • Company profile information.
  • Job vacancy details and information submitted when managing job postings.

Application Information

  • Job vacancies you apply for.
  • Information and documents submitted as part of your job applications.
  • Relevant application and recruitment records.

Technical and Usage Information

We may process certain technical information when you use the website, including browser information, device information, authentication data, and website usage information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide Jobseeker and Company services.
  • Display and manage job vacancies.
  • Process job applications.
  • Allow relevant companies to review applications and documents submitted for their vacancies.
  • Provide customer support and respond to enquiries.
  • Maintain, operate, and improve our platform.
  • Protect the platform against misuse, unauthorised access, and security issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Jobseeker and Company Accounts

Kerjiva Malaysia provides account services for Jobseekers and Companies.

Users are responsible for providing accurate information and keeping their account credentials confidential.

Jobseekers should ensure that information in their profiles, applications, and uploaded documents is accurate and appropriate for recruitment purposes.

Companies should ensure that information submitted through their accounts and job postings is accurate and does not improperly disclose another person's personal information.

4. Job Applications and Uploaded Documents

When a Jobseeker applies for a vacancy, information and documents submitted with that application may be made available to the relevant Company for recruitment and application review purposes.

Please review the information and documents you submit before completing an application.

Users should not upload documents containing information that is unnecessary for the intended recruitment process.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

Kerjiva Malaysia may use browser storage technologies to support website functionality.

For example, the website uses browser storage to remember your language preference and may use it to maintain authentication-related session information.

You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings. However, clearing certain stored information may affect website functionality or require you to sign in again.

6. Third-Party Services

Kerjiva Malaysia uses third-party services to operate certain platform features.

These services may include:

  • Supabase: Used for database, authentication, and related backend services.
  • Google Gemini: Used to provide AI-powered chatbot responses.

Information processed by these services may be subject to the respective providers' privacy policies, terms, and data-handling practices.

We encourage users to review the privacy information of these third-party providers.

7. AI Chatbot

Kerjiva Malaysia provides an AI-powered chatbot to help users obtain general information and assistance.

Messages submitted to the chatbot may be processed by an external AI service provider to generate responses.

Please do not submit passwords, payment information, confidential business information, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information through the chatbot.

AI-generated responses may not always be accurate or complete. Users should independently verify important information.

8. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through Kerjiva Malaysia against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Personal information is intended to be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable laws.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, including Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709), as amended, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information held about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Asking questions about how your information is processed.
  • Withdrawing consent where applicable and subject to legal requirements.

To make a privacy-related enquiry or request, please contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

10. Children's Privacy

Kerjiva Malaysia is intended for individuals who are legally eligible to use employment and recruitment services.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.
