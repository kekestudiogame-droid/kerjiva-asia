Safe Job Search Guide

Search smartly, safely, and confidently with Kerjiva.

Finding a job is an important step towards building your future. However, jobseekers need to be careful because fake job vacancies and various job scams can still cause financial and personal losses.

To make your job search safer, here are some useful guidelines you can follow.

1. Use Trusted Job Search Platforms

Search for job opportunities through platforms that provide clear information about job vacancies and companies. Read the complete job description before submitting your application.

2. Verify Company Information

Make sure the company name, address, website, and contact information can be found and verified. If you have doubts, contact the company through the official communication channels listed on its website.

3. Be Careful of Requests for Fees

Be cautious if anyone asks you to pay registration, interview, training, or administrative fees, or promises a job in exchange for payment. Do not rush into making any transfer.

4. Protect Your Personal Information

Never share your password, PIN, OTP code, or banking information with unauthorised parties. Only submit personal documents when they are genuinely required through a recruitment process that you can trust.

5. Verify Information Before Attending an Interview

Make sure the interview location and schedule are correct. Inform your family or someone you trust if you need to attend an in-person interview, especially at an unfamiliar location.

6. Do Not Be Easily Attracted by Unrealistic Promises

Be careful of offers with unusually high salaries but unclear requirements, recruitment processes that seem too easy, or pressure to make an immediate decision.

Remember!

A genuine job opportunity should not require jobseekers to hand over money or share confidential information carelessly. Always verify information before making a decision.
